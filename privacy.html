Hermes Career Pipeline

Privacy policy

Last updated 23 October 2026

Hermes Career Pipeline ("the application") is a personal, single-user tool that runs on its owner's own computer. This policy describes exactly what it accesses and what it does with it.

What the application accesses

  • Gmail — read access to identify messages related to job applications, and send access used only to draft messages the owner reviews and sends himself.
  • Google Drive — to store the owner's own career documents and dated backups in a folder owned by the same account.
  • Google Calendar — read access to find interview appointments.
  • Google Docs, Sheets and Contacts — only when the owner explicitly asks the application to work with a document or a contact.

Where the data goes

  • Data stays on the owner's computer and in the owner's own Google account. There is no server operated by this application and no third-party service receives the data.
  • Nothing is sold, shared, rented or used for advertising, profiling or model training.
  • Documents prepared for a job application are sent to an employer only when the owner submits them himself.

What is stored and for how long

  • Job postings, research briefs, drafts, decisions and an audit log are kept locally so the owner can review and continue his search. Local backups are retained for a bounded number of days and then deleted automatically.
  • Access tokens are stored locally on the owner's machine and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Deletion

Revoking the application's access stops all further access immediately. Local data can be deleted by the owner at any time; no copy is retained elsewhere.

Children

The application is not directed at children and has no users other than its owner.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it.
