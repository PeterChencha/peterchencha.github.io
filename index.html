Hermes Career Pipeline

A single-user job-search assistant that runs on its owner's own computer. It reads a job posting, scores it against a personal evidence library, drafts a research brief, and files the documents — with a human approving every consequential step.

What it does

  • Collects job postings from company career feeds and links its owner saves.
  • Scores each role against verified evidence; never invents experience, metrics or employers.
  • Writes research and positioning briefs, and prepares CVs and cover letters for review.
  • Keeps an audited record of every decision. Nothing is submitted without a human approval.

Who can use it

This application is not offered as a service. It runs on hardware owned by its author, for one person's job search. Google account access is granted only by the owner, for the owner.
